Hi, I’m Sean.

I live in San Francisco, where I spend a lot of time thinking about trees and transit — lately, metros around the world. You’ll also find me chasing snow on skis, building mobile games like TuBu, and working on Tableau Prep, a data prep tool, at Tableau.

As machines take over more of the everyday, it feels worth keeping a human record of it. So I’m trying to blog and journal more about day-to-day life — the small stuff worth remembering. That’s what this site is for.
